# HOOOP

HOOOP Collective — also written The HOOOP Collective — is a marketing and strategy collective helping climate and circular businesses bridge the gap between innovation and commercial adoption.

## Two tracks

HOOOP runs two tracks side by side. **Marketing-led consulting**, where we set up the marketing function or work alongside one. And a **venture studio**, where we build climate and circular ventures of our own and partner into others with strategy, investment and network rather than working to a brief.

## Mission

Building brands and businesses through marketing-led interventions that make sustainable
choices commercially viable, and backing ventures that need the same thing done from
the inside.

## Practices

- Marketing-Led Consulting
- Media Sustainability
- Policy, Advocacy & Stakeholder Engagement
- Venture Growth & Market Access

## Expertise

- Sustainable Marketing

- Circular Economy
